Saal ka kharcha kyun?

Jaise dukaan ka board aur kiraya hota hai, waise hi website ka ek naam hota hai (jaise krasasalon.in) aur use internet pe chalu rakhne ka saalana kharcha. Isse website saal bhar, din-raat chalti hai. Website ka naam aapke salon ke naam pe register hoga, aur website hamesha aapki rahegi.

Payment kaise hoga?

  • 50% advance: kaam shuru karne se pehle

  • Saalana kharcha har saal renewal ke samay

Google review ke niyam

Review card sirf review maangna aasaan banata hai. Google ke niyam:

  • Har customer se aaram se review maangna
  • Review ke badle discount, free service ya gift
  • Sirf khush customers ko review link dena
  • Salon mein dabaav, ya staff ko review ka target

Niyam todne pe Google reviews hata sakta hai.
